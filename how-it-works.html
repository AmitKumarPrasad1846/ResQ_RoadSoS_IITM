How It Works

From Crash to Care in Minutes

Simple, fast, and reliable — here's how ResQ saves lives.

1

🚗 Crash Detection

ResQ Edge hardware detects impact using accelerometer and gyroscope sensors. OR phone's motion sensors detect sudden deceleration. AI algorithms filter out false positives — only genuine crashes trigger alerts.

⚡ 0.5 sec detection 🎯 98% accuracy 🔋 72hr backup
2

📍 Automatic Location Lock

Live GPS coordinates are instantly captured. No typing. No searching. No manual input needed. Even if you're unconscious, ResQ knows exactly where you are.

📍 Live GPS 🗺️ Street-level accuracy 📱 Works without internet
3

⏱️ 30-Second Confirmation Window

ResQ gives you 30 seconds to cancel if it's a false alarm. If you don't respond — or if you're unconscious — the alert proceeds automatically to emergency services.

🛑 Cancel anytime 🤖 Auto-proceeds 💪 Unconscious? No problem
4

🚨 Alert to Command Center

Your location, timestamp, and crash data are sent instantly to ResQ Command Center. Dispatchers see your exact location on map and nearest available services.

📡 Real-time alerts 🗺️ Live map view 👥 Multiple dispatchers
5

🏥 Smart Dispatch

Nearest ambulance, police station, and trauma center are notified simultaneously. One-click dispatch sends help directly to your exact location.

🚑 Ambulance 👮 Police 🏥 Hospital
6

❤️ Rescue Begins

Help is on the way. You receive real-time updates on arrival time. Emergency services have your medical profile (if you chose to share) for better care.

📱 Real-time tracking 📋 Medical profile ✅ Rescue confirmed
