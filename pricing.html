Yes! You can cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings. No cancellation fees. Your plan remains active until the end of your billing period.
Does ResQ work without internet? +
Yes! ResQ works on 2G networks and can function offline. The app is lightweight and designed for Indian network conditions.
Is ResQ Edge hardware included? +
ResQ Edge hardware is included only in the Family and Enterprise plans. It can also be purchased separately for ₹2,999.

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and net banking.
